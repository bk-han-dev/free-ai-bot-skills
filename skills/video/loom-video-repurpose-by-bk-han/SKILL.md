# Loom Video Repurpose By BK Han

Turn one Loom recording into reusable assets, drafted for the owner's approval.

## Inputs
- The Loom video link, or its transcript or notes pasted into chat.
- The audience and goal if given (teach, sell, onboard, follow up).

## Steps
1. **Get the source.** Loom has no connector. Open the video in the browser, or use the transcript the owner gives you. At any login, 2FA or captcha wall, stop and ask the owner. Never guess what a video says.
2. **Summarise.** Write 3 to 5 key points and one core promise, in plain words.
3. **Package the video.** Draft a title (under 60 characters, hook first), a 2 to 3 line description with one CTA, and chapter markers taken from real timestamps only.
4. **Folder.** Suggest which library folder it belongs in (for example Tutorials, Bot Builds, Client Onboarding, Meetup Follow-ups, SKOOL Lessons, Sales Explainers).
5. **Repurpose.** Draft each of these in the owner's voice:
   - LinkedIn post: hook, 3 to 5 short lines of value, CTA.
   - Newsletter blurb: 60 to 100 words plus the link.
   - Community lesson: title, objective, 3 to 5 steps, one action task.
   - 2 to 3 short-clip ideas with timestamps and hook lines.
6. **Check.** Run the pre-publish QA checklist if one is available. Use official brand and framework spellings, make no invented numbers, view counts or results, and add a disclaimer when a third-party product name appears.
7. **Approval gate.** Show everything to the owner as DRAFTS, with a numbered list (1) Title and description, 2) Chapters, 3) LinkedIn, 4) Newsletter, 5) Lesson, 6) Clips, 7) All) so they can approve by number.
8. **After approval only.** Apply approved titles, descriptions or folder moves in Loom, and hand approved drafts to the matching teammate (social, newsletter, community). Never publish, share, delete, change privacy settings or invite viewers without the owner's explicit OK.

## Output
One message with the numbered drafts, followed by the approval options.

## Sample test prompts
- "Repurpose this Loom: <link>"
- "Here's the transcript of my bot-build walkthrough, make the LinkedIn post and SKOOL lesson."
- "Give me titles and chapters for my last 3 Loom videos."
- "Which folder should this onboarding Loom go in?"
