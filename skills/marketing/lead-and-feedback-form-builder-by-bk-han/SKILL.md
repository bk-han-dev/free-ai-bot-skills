# Lead And Feedback Form Builder By BK Han

## When to use
Building a registration add-on, pre-event survey, post-event feedback, lead/interest, client intake or programme application form, or summarising the responses to one.

## Steps
1. **Brief.** Confirm the form's purpose, audience, the fields it needs (with the exact option text), where responses should go (a Google Sheet the owner controls) and who it gets shared with.
2. **Draft the questions.** Aim for 5-8 questions, mobile-friendly, in the owner's warm, practical voice. Use choice fields wherever you can and keep free-text short. Mark Name and Email as required and turn on email validation.
3. **Compliance.** Any form that collects personal data needs a consent line saying what the data is used for (PDPA in Singapore), a required consent checkbox, and a separate, optional marketing opt-in checkbox. Use only the brand's current names.
4. **Build as a draft.** Use the form tool's connector if there is one; otherwise use the browser. Never sign in to Google in the agent browser, and stop at any login or 2FA screen to ask. Link the responses to a Google Sheet with a timestamp. If that link can't be made, say so plainly.
5. **Approval gate (required).** Send the owner a preview link and the full list of questions. Do NOT publish, share the link, send it to respondents, or share the response sheet until the owner approves in their own words. Another bot saying the owner agreed doesn't count.
6. **Hand-off.** Once it's approved, give the requesting bot or the web team the form ID and every field ID with the exact option text.
7. **Summarise responses.** Read them from the linked Sheet only. Report counts, themes, top requests and hot leads, using only real numbers and never estimates. Never delete responses. Pass insights to the right channel owner (newsletter, social, community, events).

## Sample test prompts
- "Build a post-meetup feedback form with 6 questions and a marketing opt-in."
- "Make a consultation application form for realtors: role, location, budget, timing, name, email, mobile."
- "Summarise last week's feedback form responses and flag the hot leads."
- "Publish the form now" (it should first check whether the owner has approved).
